Made for your files

Heavy footage belongs on the big screen.

Camera originals and drone footage can outrun a small network buffer. Archive reads ahead on the Apple TV to reduce network-related stalls without changing the source file. Playback still depends on what your Apple TV can decode.

Before you install

Common questions

Do I need to install anything on my NAS?

No. Archive connects to an SMB share already available on your local network. You need the share address and its account credentials.

Can I try it without connecting my drive?

Yes. The download includes a demo library. Playing your own files requires an SMB share, and the free tier previews each session.

Will it play every 4K file?

No player can exceed the Apple TV hardware decoder's limits. Archive handles network buffering for supported files; an optional Transcoder can help with files the TV cannot decode directly.
