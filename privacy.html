Archive — Home Video Player · Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This policy covers Archive for Android TV / Google TV and Apple TV. The sections below describe each platform separately.

Android TV and Google TV

Archive plays media from the network drives you connect. It uses SMB, a network file-sharing protocol, to access those files. Saved network passwords are encrypted on the TV using a key protected by Android Keystore. Network settings and the library index are kept on the device. Archive does not require an Archive account.

Google Play purchases and diagnostic data

Archive Pro purchases on Android are handled by Google Play. Archive receives purchase status and transaction information to manage Pro access. It does not receive your payment card details.

The Android app includes Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. This library collects and sends technical diagnostic data to Google, including Billing response codes, error messages, limited exception information, app and library versions, Android version, device model and build information, total device memory, and temporary identifiers used to correlate Billing operations. Its data transport component also includes network type, language, locale, time zone, and mobile operator information when available.

Billing connects when the app starts, so this diagnostic reporting can occur even if you do not make a purchase. Archive has no setting to disable it. The data is used for diagnostic analytics and is disclosed as collected and shared with Google. Reports can be queued on the device before transmission; this is not described as memory-only, ephemeral processing. Diagnostic reports are sent to Google's endpoint over HTTPS, which encrypts them in transit.

Google handles the information it receives under its Privacy Policy. Archive does not set Google's retention period or provide an in-app way to erase reports already received by Google. For questions about data handled by Google, see its privacy controls. You can also contact us at the address below.

Your files and local data

Playing your own files requires access to the network share you choose. Optional local companion-server and web-remote features communicate over your home network. Saved app data can be removed by clearing Archive's storage in Android settings. This does not delete files on your network drive or data already received by Google.

The Android diagnostic reporting described above is automatic and also applies when the app is used by a child. The Apple TV statements below about analytics and purchases apply only to the Apple TV version.

Apple TV version

Archive is a media player for your own files. It is built so your data stays yours: we do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything the app knows lives on your Apple TV and never goes anywhere else.

How the app works — and what network access it uses

Archive connects to computers and NAS drives on your own local network using the SMB file-sharing protocol (the same standard Windows, macOS, and NAS devices use to share folders). It uses this access for exactly one purpose: listing and streaming the media files you point it at. The app talks only to the devices you add and to Apple's App Store services for purchases. It has no server of ours to talk to — there is no "cloud" side of Archive at all.

What is stored on the device — locally and encrypted

  • Share credentials (the username and password for your own computer or NAS) are stored only in the Apple TV's secure Keychain, encrypted by the operating system. They are used solely to open connections to your own devices and are never transmitted anywhere else.
  • Network share details, favorites, watch history, and your library index are stored on-device and encrypted at rest (AES-GCM, with the encryption key held in the Keychain). They are never uploaded, synced, or shared — no one, including us, can access them.
  • The playback cache (temporary copies of files while they stream from your shares) lives on the Apple TV and can be cleared any time in Settings. It never leaves the device.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection — the app works entirely without identifying you.
  • No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising, no fingerprinting. Nothing about you or your usage is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your media never leaves your network. Streaming happens directly between your Apple TV and your own devices.

Analytics — only ever with your consent

Today the app includes no analytics of any kind. If a future version offers optional, anonymous usage statistics to help improve the app, they will be strictly opt-in: nothing will be collected unless you explicitly agree and accept, the choice will be clearly presented, and saying no (or nothing) will change nothing about how the app works.

Purchases

Optional purchases (Archive Pro) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive only Apple's anonymized transaction confirmation — never your payment details or identity.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
