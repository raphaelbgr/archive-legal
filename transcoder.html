Archive Transcoder

Version 1.0.3

Archive plays straight off your drive and needs nothing installed. The Transcoder is the exception you only want for the heavy files — 4K, HEVC, high-bitrate drone footage — that a television cannot decode smoothly on its own. It runs on a computer you already have, on your own network, and hands the TV a stream it can play.

It never talks to the internet and nothing is uploaded anywhere. The computer running it and the TV watching it have to be on the same network, and that is the whole arrangement.

Download .exe

After it is running

  1. Open the app. It shows this computer's address and a six-digit pairing code.
  2. Add the folders holding your photos and videos, or map your NAS shares in config.json.
  3. On the TV: Archive › Settings › Playback › Stream server › Pair…, then type the address and the code.

That is the whole setup. The TV finds it from then on, and a transcoder icon appears in the top bar whenever it is reachable.

About ffmpeg

ffmpeg is not bundled — it is 120 MB and licensed separately. The first launch that cannot find one downloads it for you, from BtbN on Windows and Linux and from evermeet on macOS. If you already have ffmpeg on your PATH, it uses that instead and downloads nothing.

If the TV cannot see it

  • Both machines have to be on the same network. A guest SSID is a different network.
  • On Windows, a firewall prompt that was dismissed writes a block rule for that exact file. Allow it, or remove the rule and launch again.
  • The app's own window says whether it is serving and on which address — start there rather than from the TV.
